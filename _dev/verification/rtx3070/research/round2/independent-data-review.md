## Must fix

1. **`r2-cancha-horarios`: contradictory exchange cost in the counterfactual.** `evidence[budget]` says “cambiar cuesta cero” and remains fixed. `counterfactual.replace_evidence.preference` then requires 10 compensation, and its justification prices A at 20. The rendered counterfactual therefore simultaneously supplies zero exchange cost and a surcharge. `options.A.consequence` also always says playing costs 10 total if accepted, which is false when the exchange is accepted with compensation. Express any fixed administrative fee separately from compensation and make the consequence valid under both worlds. The preference and requested compensation also move together; explicitly establish their causal connection if this is intended as one variable rather than two independent interventions.

2. **`r2-garantia-traduccion`: counterfactual A relies on an unstated distinction.** `evidence[terms]` says a failure requires a correction costing 150; it never says this must be an external, separately paid correction. The counterfactual offers free revision by P1, while `counterfactual.justification` newly calls the correction “externa” and asserts the revision cannot cover it. A reader can reasonably interpret free revision as avoiding that correction cost. Because exact failure probabilities are unknown, the relative expected costs are then not determined. State whether the required correction remains an unavoidable third-party expense and what the promised revision covers, or acknowledge that this label is ambiguous. The clean reimbursement calculation itself is coherent if all relevant failure cost is 150.

3. **`r2-intercambio-vinilo`: the counterfactual creates a useful waiting option inside the offered negotiation action.** `counterfactual.replace_evidence.offer` makes the rival offer expire in five minutes; the alternative from P2 remains available until tomorrow (`situation`), and `objective` minimizes payment without a requirement to close immediately. `options.A.action` retains P2 while proposing 8 to P1. No negotiation cost or lost fallback is given. Waiting briefly/proposing 8 after expiry can therefore preserve the fallback while potentially obtaining 8, whereas B commits to 10. The justification that another round is incompatible with the written price is supported only while the rival offer is active. Clarify whether the rival transaction closes irreversibly, whether the relevant price cannot change within the decision window, or whether A is explicitly an immediate one-shot attempt. Current B is reasonable, but not uniquely justified by the stated objective and timing.

4. **`r2-huerto-portavoz`: counterfactual justification silently drops the deadline.** `objective` requires distributing plots today with valid agreements. Counterfactual B reserves them and requests consent tomorrow. It respects authority and is the sensible fallback among these actions, but does not obtain the same stated outcome today. `counterfactual.justification` presents it as obtaining the agreement required by the same objective without acknowledging this failure. Keep the objective fixed and explicitly state that no offered action satisfies both requirements in that world, and that B sacrifices the deadline to preserve validity. A label flip alone is insufficient evidence of satisfying the fixed objective.

5. **`r2-entrada-reventa`: a relational consequence becomes false in the counterfactual.** `options.A.relational_cost` says P1 loses the opportunity to buy an available ticket. Counterfactual `transfer` explicitly makes that ticket unusable by P1, so choosing A no longer deprives P1 of that usable opportunity. The renderer copies this assertion into every variant's target. Qualify it by actual transferability or remove that asserted cost. Also clarify option C's “exception” versus `evidence[refund]`'s express refusal: the target still gives C a possible recovery of 12, while its reasoning treats recovery as unsupported. If a costless last request remains possible without jeopardizing the recital, A versus C is less decisive than the target suggests.

6. **`r2-cena-indirecta`: clean uncertainty/revision fields assume the counterfactual vehicle.** Clean `evidence[transport]` expressly says no other Friday transport exists, but `revision_conditions` says to revisit if “se cancela el coche.” `missing_information` also refers to confirmed transport, although only the counterfactual confirms a car. These fields are inherited by clean/noise/cosmetic variants. Make baseline fields describe the bus constraint and use the supported counterfactual overrides for the confirmed car. The action labels themselves follow the explicit home-dinner preference coherently.

7. **Several target consequences introduce decisive logistics that the model never sees.** `data.py:variant` includes only option **actions** in the user prompt, while consequence fields are target outputs. `r2-garantia-traduccion.options.C.consequence` asserts that delaying one day loses Monday's hiring deadline, but no date or last hiring time appears in the evidence. `r2-errata-audiencia.options.B.consequence` asserts forty individual calls exceed noon, without time remaining or call durations. `r2-club-silencio.options.C.consequence` asserts both reservations expire today; `evidence[feasible]` only says both plans are available. These outcomes may be plausible, but certainty is unsupported from the rendered inputs. Put the logistics in evidence if they are scenario facts, or make the forecasts explicitly uncertain. This is a target-grounding problem even when another action remains preferable.

## Suggestions and limitations

- **Single-variable scope:** all counterfactuals replace exactly one evidence key and preserve the stored objective/horizon/options. That is a structural pass, not proof of one semantic intervention. Besides the cancha bundle above, `r2-cabina-interprete` and `r2-ensayo-sustituta` switch both access and explicit acknowledgment; `r2-telescopio-reputacion` switches both history and the author's risk estimate. These can represent one information/risk state, but the author should describe that scope rather than claiming one changed atomic fact.
- **Weak distractors:** C is often an obviously inferior postponement, budget violation, or cancellation, e.g. `r2-quorum-archivo`, `r2-licencia-repetida`, `r2-cancha-horarios`, and `r2-huerto-portavoz`. This is acceptable for a simple curriculum slice but limits evidence of nuanced social discrimination. Do not add complexity merely to change difficulty metrics.
- **No deterministic length cue found:** option action lengths are close (roughly 40–62 characters), selected actions are not consistently longest, and the renderer shuffles positions per family and cosmetic variant. Repeated unused people/names and three-option templates may still permit template learning; this review cannot measure that from 16 families.
- **Unsupported mental-state certainty is generally avoided:** explanations commonly distinguish missing access, logistics, or uncertain motivation from deliberate deception. `r2-club-silencio` appropriately preserves action while lowering stated confidence; 6/10 is a weak empirical preference, not a calibrated attendance probability. Likewise `r2-testigos-parada` supplies risk calibration rather than deriving it from raw message count.
- **Provenance:** all families declare owned-synthetic authorship, date, no external dataset examples, and pending-human review. These are consistent metadata assertions; they do not independently prove originality or source isolation. Preserve pending review status until the relevant review process actually completes.
- **Domain/horizon:** eight work and eight personal contexts are sensible; choir, club, garden, and private trading scenarios read as personal. The horizon field is not included in the compact model prompt by this renderer, so fixed stored horizons alone do not show that the model reasons over an explicitly supplied horizon. The licensing objective does include “durante el contrato,” allowing its guaranteed future-margin comparison despite the broad `short` tag.

No candidate labels or content were edited. Root should reconcile these findings with the author and bind admission to the final revised candidate hash.
