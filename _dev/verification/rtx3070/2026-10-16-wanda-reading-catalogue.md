## Transform sources into useful training

For each admitted passage, create an attributed concept record with limits of applicability; independently authored examples where the technique works; counterexamples where it fails; a causal counterfactual; irrelevant-noise variants; and concise evidence-linked recommendations. Keep a whole source-derived causal family in one split. Do not put a book paraphrase in training and its translation in the sealed benchmark.

Distinguish observable behavior, reports, hypotheses and predictions. Evaluate sales and relationships against the user's stated objectives, time horizon and costs. Do not reward persuasion merely because it sounds persuasive, or label a recommendation good because a book author said it. Compare outcomes in development and retain failures.

No moral moderation layer is introduced. Evidence quality and historical attribution prevent unsupported claims from becoming ground truth; Core/Cortex already owns execution and authority boundaries.

## Expanded bibliography

The owner's scope includes mentalism, suggestion, hypnosis, deception, power and darker influence tactics. A source is not excluded merely because it discusses those topics. Catalogue status is based on provenance, rights and evidential use, not a moral score. Distinguish original Carnegie works, later revisions and publications by Dale Carnegie Training; do not attribute all branded works to Dale himself. De-duplicate shared chapters/editions before sampling. Expand this bibliography as verified sources are found; this first expansion is not an assertion of completeness.
